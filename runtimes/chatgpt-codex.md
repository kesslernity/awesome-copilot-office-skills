# ChatGPT and Codex

## ChatGPT

In the sidebar select **Plugins**, open the **Skills** tab, select **Create**, then **Upload from your computer**, and upload `dist/personal/<skill>.zip`. ChatGPT scans uploaded skills before they become available. Invoke with `@` and the skill name, or let ChatGPT match the description.

Sources, read 2026-10-24: [Skills in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001066-skills-in-chatgpt), [Build skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills).

## Codex

Copy the skill folder to `.agents/skills/<skill>/` in a repository, or `~/.agents/skills/<skill>/` for all projects. Invoke with `$` and the skill name, or `/skills`. Source: [Build skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills).
